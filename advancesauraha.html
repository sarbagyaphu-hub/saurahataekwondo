01 / THE CHAMPIONSHIP

One arena.
Many nations.
One spirit.

The championship is organized to promote Taekwondo development, sports tourism and international friendship through martial arts.

Competitors will participate under World Taekwondo and Nepal Taekwondo Association modified rules, with Kyorugi and Individual Poomsae divisions across official age categories.

01

Competition Format

WT & NTA modified rules, single elimination, with Best-of-3 rounds applied in Kyorugi.

02

Protective Equipment

WT-approved PSS where available, Daedo-approved electronic trunk protector and headgear, plus required personal safety equipment.

03

Scoring

Senior Kyorugi divisions use full PSS. Sub-Junior divisions may use Half PSS according to the organizing committee arrangement.

04

Coach Conduct

Formal sports attire, accreditation and professional conduct are required. Jeans, slippers, smoking or intoxication are not permitted.
